PRACTICAL - 1 outcome(what we learn or challenge face)

In this practical we learn about different word of web development like wireframe , sitemap , requirement analysis.
we also learn about different between git and GitHub.

Wireframe is a blueprint of a web. We can imagine how our website will look.
Sitemap is a workflow structure of whole website that how all pages are inter-connected.
Requirement analysis is a expectation or we can say demand of client that what they need in a website.

we also learn how to create a repository in GitHub and upload our work.
